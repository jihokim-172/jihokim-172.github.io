LineShine and HANGANG: the TOP500 No. 1 and Korea’s No. 1

Nov. 28, 2026 · Supercomputer

LineShine has led the TOP500 since June 2026 and kept the top spot on the November 2026 list announced at SC26. Korea’s fastest system on the same list is HANGANG at KISTI, which entered at No. 12. LineShine delivers 6.4 times the HPL performance of HANGANG and draws 7.3 times the power.† LineShine is CPU-only, while HANGANG is GPU-accelerated.

ISCA, MICRO, HPCA papers, 2008–2026

Nov. 24, 2026 · Conferences

Papers per year

  • The three conferences together grew 4.4×. They published 116 papers in 2008 and 514 in 2026.
  • Most of that growth is recent. The first doubling took twelve years (116 in 2008, 218 in 2020). The next one took six (514 in 2026). More than half of the whole 18-year increase came in 2025 and 2026 alone.
  • For a decade the size barely moved. From 2008 to 2017 each conference stayed between roughly 40 and 60 papers. A first step up came around 2018–2020, to the 60–80 range.
  • The jump did not land in the same year. ISCA and HPCA jumped in 2025 (87 → 135 and 81 → 121). ISCA kept growing in 2026 (172), while HPCA stayed flat (122). MICRO grew steadily until 2025 (123) and then jumped to 220 in 2026.
  • One conference is now the size of three. MICRO 2026 alone (220 papers) is as large as ISCA, MICRO, and HPCA combined in 2020 (218).

ISCA papers by topic, 2008–2025

  • AI accelerators are the one topic that grew with the conference. ISCA had 3 such papers in total from 2008 to 2015. It had 9 in 2016 and 38 in 2025, which is 28% of that year’s program.
  • That one topic explains a large part of ISCA’s growth. ISCA grew by 98 papers between 2008 and 2025, and AI accelerators account for 38 of them.
  • CPU and DRAM did not shrink; they stood still. CPU papers stayed around 5–10 a year and DRAM papers around 4–10. Because the program grew, their share fell: CPU was 31% of ISCA 2009 and 8% of ISCA 2025.
  • Security roughly tripled. 13 papers in 2008–2016 and 41 in 2017–2025, the period that follows Spectre and Meltdown.
  • GPU papers peaked, dipped, and came back. They rose to 7 in 2015, fell to 1–3 a year in 2020–2024, and returned to 9 in 2025.
  • Storage and networking stay niche at ISCA. SSD/NAND has 15 papers in 18 years and never more than 3 in a year. Interconnect had 12 papers in 2008–2011 and 17 in the fourteen years since.

Source: DBLP proceedings listings; MICRO 2026 from the conference program. ISCA topics are assigned from paper titles by keyword.
